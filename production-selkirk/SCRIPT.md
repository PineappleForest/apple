## Facts (with sources)
- 1704: Selkirk, sailing master of the Cinque Ports, judged the ship unseaworthy and asked to be left on Más a Tierra
  (Juan Fernández Islands, ~420 miles off Chile); Captain Stradling left him. The Cinque Ports later sank and most of
  her crew died or were taken prisoner by the Spanish.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Selkirk
  - https://www.worldhistory.org/Alexander_Selkirk/
- Rats gnawed his feet and clothes while he slept, so he fed the feral cats goat meat until they grew so tame they
  "would lie about in hundreds" and freed him of the rats. He tamed young goats too and "would now and then sing and
  dance with them and his cats." (Woodes Rogers, *A Cruising Voyage Round the World*, 1712)
  - https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/maritime-history/curatorial/dealing-self-isolation-desert-island-guide-real-robinson-crusoe
  - https://dynamic-rsnz.natlib.govt.nz/newspapers/TAN18890529.2.57 (reprint of Rogers' account)
- Rescued 2 February 1709 by Woodes Rogers' Duke, after four years and four months alone. He outran Rogers' bulldog
  and fastest men catching goats for the crew.
  - https://dynamic-rsnz.natlib.govt.nz/newspapers/TAN18890529.2.57
  - https://britishheritage.com/timeline-february-2-1709
- "He had so much forgot his Language for want of Use, that we could scarce understand him, for he seem'd to speak his
  words by halves." (Rogers, 1712)
  - https://britishheritage.com/timeline-february-2-1709
- Depiction: Selkirk in goatskins, bearded (a beard is right here, he is a man); cats and goats as fx cards like
  `fxPig`; the sinking ship stays a distant silhouette, no deaths on screen.

## Script (159 counted words)
1. In seventeen oh-four, a Scottish sailor was left alone on an island, and ended up dancing with cats.
2. Alexander Selkirk told his captain the ship was going to sink.
3. The captain said: then stay here.
4. He was right. The ship sank. He was also alone.
5. At night, rats chewed his feet while he slept.
6. So he fed the wild cats goat meat. Then more cats. Soon, hundreds of them slept around him.
7. He tamed baby goats too. And to pass the time, he sang and danced with all of them.
8. Four years and four months later, a ship finally came. Selkirk outran their bulldog to catch them dinner.
9. But when he spoke, the sailors could barely understand him. He'd forgotten how to talk.
10. Cats keep away the rats. They don't keep away the silence.
11. If your days have gone quiet, talk to Jenny on Feeloy. She always talks back.
12. No goats required.
13. Search Feeloy on the App Store.
