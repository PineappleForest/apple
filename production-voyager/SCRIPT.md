# "One Last Look": Voyager 1 and the Pale Blue Dot (Feeloy short, ~25 s)

## Facts (checked 2026-11-17)
- 14 Feb 1990: Voyager 1, ~6 billion km out, turned its camera back and took the 60-frame "Family Portrait"; Earth is
  0.12 of a pixel in the "Pale Blue Dot" frame. Sagan had lobbied for years; the team had refused several times (Sun
  near the field of view, limited resources). Earth frames at 04:48 GMT; cameras switched off 34 min later to save
  power, and never used again (NASA account; Wikipedia "Pale Blue Dot"; Planetary Society).
- Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth (25.9 billion km) on 18 Nov 2026 (NASA/JPL via EarthSky, IFLScience,
  ScienceDaily 3 Nov 2026): a signal takes 24 h each way. It is still operating, with instruments being switched off.
- Wording choices: "asked for one favour" compresses years of lobbying (true in spirit); "a hello takes a whole day to
  reach it" stays true after 18 Nov, so the video doesn't date. Launch 1977, not mentioned.
